Enboku Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Kumori ("we") describes here how the game "Enboku" (the "App") handles information.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, contact details or location. The App does not send your play records to our servers.

2. Data stored on your device

Your play records (scores, progress, settings, chosen looks) are stored only on your device. Deleting the App deletes them.

3. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to show ads. To do so, AdMob (Google) may collect from your device the advertising identifier, approximate location (from your IP address), ad impressions and interactions, and crash and performance data.
The App does not use personalized ads and does not ask for permission to track you.
See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information for ads.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases of "Hyakuban" are processed by Apple's App Store. We never receive your payment information. You can restore purchases from the purchase screen or Settings.

5. Game Center

Leaderboards use Apple Game Center. Taking part is optional. Your Game Center nickname and scores are shown through Apple's service; we do not receive them.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes

We will announce any changes to this policy on this page.
